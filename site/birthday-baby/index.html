Good to know

How many photos can I add?

Each design comes with a set number of slides that you can duplicate or delete in Canva, so you can make it as long or as short as you like.

How do I send a video invitation?

Download the finished MP4 from Canva to your phone, then share it like any other video by text, WhatsApp, email or social media.

Can I print it myself?

Yes. Download a PDF from Canva and print at home or at any print shop.

How do I get my template after I buy it?

Complete your purchase on Etsy, then download the PDF file sent to your email. Click the Canva link inside the PDF to open your template and start personalizing it.

Do I need design skills?

No. Click any text to change it, drag in your photos, and download. If you get stuck, message the shop and we will help.
