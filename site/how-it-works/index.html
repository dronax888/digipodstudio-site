How it works

From choosing a design to playing it at the party.

  1. Pick your design. Browse by celebration or by color. Tap the heart to keep favorites in your party list and share the list with family.
  2. Order on Etsy. Every Buy button goes to our Etsy shop. Etsy handles payment, so you can use the cards and wallets you already trust there.
  3. Get your download. It is ready as soon as payment goes through and gives you access to your template in Canva.
  4. Personalize it. In Canva, click any text to change it, drag in your own photos and video clips, and pick your music.
  5. Share or play it. Download an MP4 for videos and slideshows, publish website invitations to a free link, or download images and PDFs for printing.

Questions

How do I get my template after I buy it?

You check out on Etsy, which handles the payment. Your digital download is ready right away and gives you access to your template in Canva.

Do I need design skills?

No. Click any text to change it, drag in your photos, and download. If you get stuck, message the shop and we will help.

Can I edit on my phone?

Yes. Canva works in the browser and in the free Canva app on iPhone and Android, though a laptop is easier for long slideshows.

Can I write it in Spanish?

Yes. Every word is editable, so you can use Spanish, English or both, and several designs come bilingual already.

Is anything shipped to me?

No. These are digital templates. Nothing is mailed, so there is no waiting and no shipping cost.

What if I need help?

Message the shop on Etsy. Buyers mention fast, friendly replies in their reviews.
