Questions about website invitations

How does the RSVP work?

Designs that include RSVP link to a form where guests answer, and you see every reply in one place. Look for "Online RSVP" in the details.

Do I need a website domain?

No. Canva publishes the site on a free link. You can connect your own domain later if you want one.

Can I change the site after I share it?

Yes. Edit it in Canva and publish again. Guests keep using the same link.
