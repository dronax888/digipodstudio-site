Good to know

How long is the teaser?

It is short and made for stories and reels. You can change the timing of each scene in Canva.

Will it fit our screen?

It is widescreen, the shape of most TVs and projectors. Test it on the venue screen before the day.

What sizes can I print?

You can resize the design in Canva before downloading. Check with your print shop for the paper sizes they offer.

How do I get my template after I buy it?

Complete your purchase on Etsy, then download the PDF file sent to your email. Click the Canva link inside the PDF to open your template and start personalizing it.

Do I need design skills?

No. Click any text to change it, drag in your photos, and download. If you get stuck, message the shop and we will help.
