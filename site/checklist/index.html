Quinceañera planning checklist

Print it, stick it on the fridge and tick it off as a family. Pair it with the party planner to get the exact dates for your celebration.

12 to 18 months before

  • Set the date and the budget as a family
  • Book the church or ceremony and the reception venue
  • Book the photographer, videographer and DJ or band
  • Choose the padrinos and the court of honor (damas and chambelanes)
  • Choose her colors and theme
  • Start shopping for the dress

6 to 8 months before

  • Send the save the date
  • Book the caterer and the cake
  • Reserve a hotel block for guests who travel
  • Start choreography rehearsals for the waltz and surprise dance
  • Order the dress, the tiara and the shoes

3 months before

  • Publish the website invitation with the RSVP form
  • Book flowers, decor and the photo booth
  • Plan the program: mass, entrance, waltz, toast, traditions
  • Choose the last doll, the shoes for the shoe ceremony and the gifts

6 to 8 weeks before

  • Send the video invitation and printed cards for elders
  • Start collecting photos for the slideshow
  • Confirm the menu and the cake design
  • Final dress fitting

3 weeks before

  • RSVP deadline: chase the late replies
  • Give the final head count to the venue and caterer
  • Make the seating plan

2 weeks before

  • Finish the slideshow and download the MP4
  • Confirm the timeline with every vendor
  • Prepare envelopes with vendor tips

The week of the party

  • Test the slideshow on the venue screen
  • Pack an emergency kit: safety pins, sewing kit, phone charger, snacks
  • Rehearse the entrance and the waltz one last time

After the party

  • Send thank-you messages to guests and padrinos
  • Share the slideshow video with family who could not come
  • Back up all photos and videos in two places
