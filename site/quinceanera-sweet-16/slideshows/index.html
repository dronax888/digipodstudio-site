Questions about slideshows

How many photos can I add?

Each design comes with a set number of slides that you can duplicate or delete in Canva, so you can make it as long or as short as you like.

Can I add our own song?

Yes. Canva lets you upload your own audio file or choose from its music library, and you can trim it to fit.

What size is the video?

The slideshow is widescreen, the shape of most TVs, projectors and venue screens. Download it from Canva as an MP4.
