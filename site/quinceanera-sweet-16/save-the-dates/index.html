Questions about save the dates

When should I send a save the date?

For a quinceañera or wedding, six to eight months before is common, and earlier if many guests travel.

Is it a video or a picture?

It depends on the design. Animated and musical designs download as an MP4 video, and you can also save a still image of any page.

Can I match it to my invitation?

Yes. Search the shop by your colors to find invitations and slideshows in the same palette.
