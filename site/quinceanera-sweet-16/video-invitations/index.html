Questions about video invitations

How do I send a video invitation?

Download the finished MP4 from Canva to your phone, then share it like any other video by text, WhatsApp, email or social media.

Can I write it in Spanish?

Yes. Every word is editable, so you can write the whole invitation in Spanish, English or both.

Do guests need an app to open it?

No. It is a normal video file that plays on any phone.
