Privacy

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not collect payment details. Purchases happen on Etsy, under Etsy's privacy policy.

If you sign up for our checklist and emails, we keep your email address, the celebration you chose and the party date (if you gave one) in a private Google Sheet that only Digipod Studio can see. We use it only to send you planning ideas and new designs, at most once a week. To unsubscribe or have your details deleted, reply to any email or write to the address below.

Your party list is stored only in your own browser on your device. Sharing a list creates a link containing the template numbers you chose and nothing else.

Product photos load from Etsy's image servers and fonts load from Google Fonts. Those services receive standard technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests them.

Questions: support@digipodstudio.com. Last updated November 7, 2026.
