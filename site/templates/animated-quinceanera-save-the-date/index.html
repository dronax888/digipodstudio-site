About this design

An editable save the date, animated or musical, that announces the date months ahead. Personalize it in Canva and download it as a video or image.

Send it six months or more before the party so family can plan travel, then follow up with the full invitation.

How it works

  1. Purchase. Complete your purchase on Etsy.

  4. Save & share. Download your finished video and share it with your guests.

Questions

When should I send a save the date?

For a quinceañera or wedding, six to eight months before is common, and earlier if many guests travel.

Is it a video or a picture?

It depends on the design. Animated and musical designs download as an MP4 video, and you can also save a still image of any page.

Can I match it to my invitation?

Yes. Search the shop by your colors to find invitations and slideshows in the same palette.

How do I get my template after I buy it?

Complete your purchase on Etsy, then download the PDF file sent to your email. Click the Canva link inside the PDF to open your template and start personalizing it.
