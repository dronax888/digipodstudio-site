Most families no longer choose one or the other. They send a digital invitation to most guests and print a few cards for the people who will treasure them. Here is what each option does well.

## Video invitations

A short animated invitation with her photo and music, sent as an MP4.

**Best for:** family group chats, WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook stories.

**Why families like them:** they are shared instantly, cost nothing to send and are watched again and again. Guests abroad receive them at the same moment as everyone else.

**Keep in mind:** a video cannot hold every detail. Pair it with a website invitation for addresses, times and the RSVP.

## Invitation websites

A one-page site with the schedule, maps, dress code, hotel information and an RSVP form.

**Best for:** collecting RSVPs and answering every question in one place.

**Why families like them:** you can update details after sending, and all the replies arrive in one list.

**Keep in mind:** older relatives may need a hand opening a link, so add a QR code to any printed card.

## Printed invitations

Cards mailed or delivered by hand.

**Best for:** grandparents, padrinos and anyone who will keep the invitation as a memento.

**Keep in mind:** printing and postage take time and money, and changes mean reprinting.

## The combination that works for most families

1. Send a **save the date video** six to eight months ahead.
2. Publish the **invitation website** with the RSVP about ten weeks out.
3. Send the **video invitation** to everyone six to eight weeks before, with the website link.
4. Print a **small number of cards** for grandparents and padrinos, with a QR code to the website.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it acceptable to send quinceañera invitations by text?

Yes. Digital invitations are now common for quinceañeras. Print a few cards for the elders who prefer paper.

### How do guests RSVP to a digital invitation?

Through an RSVP form on the invitation website. Replies collect in one place, so you can see the head count at any time.

### Can a digital invitation be bilingual?

Yes. Every word in a Canva template is editable, so you can write it in Spanish, English or both.
